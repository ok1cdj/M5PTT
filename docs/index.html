Before you connect

  • Browser — Chrome, Edge, or Brave on a desktop or laptop. Web Serial doesn't exist in Safari, and not at all on iPhone or iPad. There's no workaround for that.
  • Cable — a USB-C cable that carries data, not a charge-only one.
  • Board — M5Stack AtomS3 Lite.
  • Bootloader mode — hold the side button, plug in the cable, keep holding for about 2 seconds after it powers up. AtomS3 Lite has no auto-reset circuit, so this manual step is needed every time you flash.

Connect & flash

This browser can't flash over USB. Open this page in Chrome, Edge, or Brave on a computer instead. This page needs to be loaded over HTTPS for the browser to allow a USB connection.

Still holding the button? Click connect, choose the matching serial port from the browser prompt, then let go.

Getting into bootloader mode

  1. Unplug the AtomS3 Lite.
  2. Press and hold the button on top.
  3. Plug in the USB-C cable while still holding.
  4. Keep holding for about 2 seconds, then release.
  5. Click “Connect device” above and pick the port.
